Privacy policy

Effective October 16, 2026

3825 IT Solutions provides Connect with Teams for Freshservice. This policy describes the unchanged Freshplug package confirmed by the publisher as the approved release.

Information used

The app reads ticket and requester information made available by Freshservice: email addresses, requester name, ticket number and CC addresses. It constructs Microsoft Teams links locally. The app does not use a publisher-operated database, identity lookup service, Microsoft Graph API, or stored Microsoft credentials.

Information passed to Microsoft

When you select an action, your browser opens teams.microsoft.com with recipient addresses in the URL. Chat links also include a greeting, requester first name and ticket number; group-chat links include the CC addresses. Meeting links include an attendee, subject and greeting referencing the ticket. Call links include the recipient address.

The app does not include the ticket description, attachments or conversation history in these generated links. Prepared text is not automatically sent as a chat message by the app; you control sending and meeting invitations in Teams. Call behavior is controlled by Teams. Links and their parameters may be visible in browser history and processed by Microsoft under your Microsoft agreements.

Hosting and resources

Freshworks hosts the app and provides its client SDK. The app loads Crayons UI resources from jsDelivr. These resource providers receive browser request metadata such as IP address and user agent. No requester data is intentionally added to CDN resource requests. If this documentation site is hosted on GitHub Pages, GitHub processes ordinary hosting/access metadata. This site contains no analytics, contact form, payment collection or publisher-added cookies.

Retention and requests

The app has no persistent ticket cache or publisher-hosted customer database. Freshworks, Microsoft and hosting providers may retain their own records under their policies. Support emails are handled through Gmail and retained as needed to respond and meet applicable obligations. Contact jim.henry.allen@gmail.com for access, correction or deletion requests relating to support correspondence. Account administrators and platform providers handle records kept by Freshworks and Microsoft.

Your controls

Verify addresses and drafted content before sending, follow organization policies, and uninstall the app to stop using its actions. Removing the app does not erase existing Teams communications. We do not sell requester information or use it for advertising or AI training.

Setup and support

Keep Teams actions beside the requester details your agents already use.

Requirements

  • A Freshservice administrator who can install Marketplace apps.
  • Microsoft Teams access for the agent and a recipient address Teams can resolve.
  • Browser permission to open Teams links or the Teams desktop app.

Installation

  1. Locate the publisher's Connect with Teams listing in Freshworks Marketplace and install it in the intended Freshservice account. Confirm any price and terms shown there.
  2. No app API key, client secret, Microsoft OAuth consent, or installation parameters are required by the supplied version.
  3. Open a ticket and expand the app in Requester information. The app initializes when its section is opened.
  4. Choose an action and sign in to the intended Teams account if prompted.

Using the actions

  • Message: open a requester conversation with a prepared greeting and ticket number. Review and send it yourself.
  • Group chat: include the requester and ticket CC email addresses. The button appears when the app can derive at least one eligible CC display name. Display names are guesses from email addresses; review actual recipients in Teams.
  • Call: open the Teams call flow. Selecting a call action can lead to ringing the recipient; coordinate a test before using it.
  • Meeting: open a scheduling draft with an attendee, subject and ticket reference. Review details and availability in Teams before sending an invitation.

Troubleshooting

No email on file: check the ticket and requester email data. The supplied version prefers ticket email/sender email, then requester email. Confirm the destination before communicating.

No group action: no CCs may be present, or their addresses may look like generic mailboxes. More than two named CCs are abbreviated in the label.

Wrong or unavailable recipient: verify the email is the recipient's Teams identity. A matching email alone does not establish external access, federation or tenant permissions.

Unable to load—tap to retry: retry after Freshservice finishes loading; reopen the panel or refresh the ticket if necessary.

Teams does not open: inspect blocked-pop-up notices, sign in to the correct Microsoft account, and use the browser option offered by Teams where available. Desktop behavior depends on your installation and browser.

Removal

Uninstall through Freshservice Admin → Apps → Manage Apps. The supplied app has no Microsoft API authorization to revoke. Uninstalling does not delete messages or meetings already created in Teams.
